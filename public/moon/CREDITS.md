# Moon textures

- `lro-albedo-2k.jpg`, `lro-albedo-4k.jpg` — NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit
  (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720), derived from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) WAC
  mosaics. Public domain; converted to grayscale. Equirectangular, longitude 0° (near side) at the centre.
- `shaded-relief-1k.jpg` — stylised shaded-relief lunar map carried over from the prototype.
